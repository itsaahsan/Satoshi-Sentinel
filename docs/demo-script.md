# Demo script — 3 minutes

## Opening — 20s
"Bitcoin gives people financial freedom, but its public ledger can reveal more than users realize. Most people don't know their privacy risk until it's too late. Meet Satoshi Sentinel."

## 1. Scan wallet — 40s
- Click **Scan a Wallet** (demo Exposed wallet prefilled: `bc1qdemo-exposed-privacy-28`).
- **Analyze Privacy** → narrate the scan lines → score ring lands ~28/100.
- "Reused addresses and many public interactions create potential linking risks — phrased as possibilities, never accusations."

## 2. AI explanation — 40s
- Open **Sentinel AI** → ask *"Why is address reuse a privacy problem?"*
- Point out: beginner-clear answer + scan context chip (score carried in).

## 3. Transaction graph — 30s
- Show zoom/pan → click an edge → AI explanation panel appears.
- "Raw chain data becomes a story a beginner can follow."

## 4. Nostr — 30s
- **Nostr Network** → relay status → **Generate demo identity** → publish the prefilled tip.
- "Education spreads over censorship-resistant relays, not a centralized server."

## Closing — 20s
"Satoshi Sentinel helps users understand their Bitcoin footprint before someone else does — Bitcoin transparency, AI intelligence, decentralized communication, one privacy-first experience."
